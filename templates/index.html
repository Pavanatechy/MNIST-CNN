Pipeline

How It Works

From your hand stroke to neural network activation in four transparent steps.

01

Interactive Drawing

You draw on a responsive 280×280 HTML5 canvas with a smooth stroke algorithm supporting mouse and touch input.

02

MNIST Normalization

The digit bounding box is isolated, centered, and scaled to a 20×20 box within a 28×28 frame, inverted to black background, and normalized to [0, 1].

03

CNN Feature Extraction

Two 2D Convolutional layers (32 & 64 filters) with ReLU activations and MaxPooling detect edges, loops, and spatial handwriting patterns.

04

Softmax Probability

The dense classifier outputs 10 probability scores representing digits 0–9. The argmax identifies the prediction in under 15ms.
